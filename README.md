# Climate Risk & Sustainable Finance in the Indian Textile Industry

Presentation deck for **Climatonomics CIA-3**, Christ University.
A case study of the Tirupur knitwear cluster and Digsel (Selvas Garments).

**▶ Open the presentation:** https://swas204.github.io/Climatonomics_PPT/

## How to present it

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `→` / `Space` | Next slide |
| `←` | Previous slide |
| **`N`** | **Study panel** — left side explains everything on the slide, right side is how to present it |
| **`R`** | **Revision list** — one line per slide, for last-minute prep |
| `M` | Jump to any slide |
| `F` | Fullscreen |

**8 slides**, one per speaker plus a cover and the stakeholder video. Runs **14:55
excluding the video**, and each slide's time budget shows in the footer bar as you
present. Slide 6 has three green claims you can click to flip.

## Adding the stakeholder video

Slide 7 holds the 5-minute video. Save the file in this folder as exactly
**`stakeholder-video.mp4`** and it plays inline. If it's absent, the slide shows a
"play the video now" cue card instead, so you can play it from anywhere. GitHub rejects
files over 100 MB — compress it, or keep the cue card and play from a laptop.

In the activities, click an option to add a vote (`Shift`+click adds five), then
**Show the answer**. After revealing, click any dimmed option to see why it's wrong.
